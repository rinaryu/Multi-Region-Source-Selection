Defining your project
You can choose the area of your project based on your interest. Your project can be about any of the course topics you are interest on gaining more in-depth knowledge on. You can define your project in one of the following areas we cover in this course:

Network Applications & Application Layer Protocol Development
Reachability: Routing & Forwarding
Multimedia 
Network Security
Data Center Networks 
Wireless & Mobility
Your project definition should include a specific problem that is of an acceptable complexity, uses the knowledge of this course, and is manageable within the time frame (40 days in steps from project expectations availability to outcomes due). You are required to include programming and/or scripting, test network setup, and traffic measurement components to validate your project.

Your project could be based on observations you had during Interactive Sessions, your learning on a specific topic that you want to expand towards a specific direction, or any related topic to the course modules that you want to gain more in-depth knowledge about.

 

Choosing & Specifying your project
If you are choosing a project different from those listed below, please consult the teaching team, and apply the feedback received on the second step of the project and refine your definition for the third step of your project. You can also use and tweak sample project definitions provided below.

 

Implementing & Testing your project
You are required to implement your project using Python (and scripting and/or C/C++ if necessary).  You are not required to focus on specifications that are not networking related. So, please spend your time and energy on details of networking including the design of your network and service, making, sending, and receiving packets and analyzing packets you have received. You are welcome if you want to implement further application details that are not networking related, but you will not receive any marks for them. You will need to learn integrating your code with the networking environment required for the test of your project.

You are required to set up a network of senders and receivers in which you test your project. You need to come up with a required test network diagram indicating network elements (hosts, routers, etc), addresses, and protocol requirements.

 

Presenting your project &. submitting project outcomes
You will present the outcomes of your project in the class. The presentations will be peer-reviewed: Thee of your classmates, chosen at random, will review each project, and their review will be considered as one of the elements of grading the project. You are given some feedback during presentation and can make minor modification on your outcomes before your final submission based on the presentation feedback.

Sample Project Titles
Easy: You can choose from the following two projects that are considered easy:

(1) Source Selection

In this project, you design and implement a multimedia source selection mechanism for a content distribution network. Assuming your client is using DASH for streaming multimedia, and multiple sources across the Internet are providing the same content, your task is to steer the request to the appropriate server based on client request. You should run your own servers, segment your files, build your manifests, and run your client side player of choice so that you can view your files to be able to test your selection mechanism. Then, you are required to measure your traffic characteristics, and report on the quality and traffic behaviour of your source selection mechanism under different traffic conditions and user requests.
